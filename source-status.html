Every feed this digest watches, and when it last did something. All times are UTC. Last new story is the most recent item from that source that survived de-duplication and made it into a published edition. Last successful fetch is the most recent time the pipeline pulled the feed and got a usable response back — a source can be fetched successfully and still have no new story. Newest item served is the most recent publication date among everything the feed returned, which is what distinguishes a genuinely active source from a mirror that answers every request but has stopped advancing. Sources are marked quiet when they are reachable but have not delivered a story in 7 days, and frozen when everything they serve is older than that window.
