Every feed this digest watches, and when it last did something. All times are UTC. Newest item served is the most recent publication date among everything the feed returned, which is what distinguishes a genuinely active source from a mirror that answers every request but has stopped advancing. Last successful fetch is the most recent time the pipeline pulled the feed and got a usable response back — a source can be fetched successfully and still have no new story. Sources are marked quiet when they are reachable but have not delivered a story in 7 days, and frozen when everything they serve is older than that window.
